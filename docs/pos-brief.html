What we want built

A Bhutan shop POS that is cheaper than YetiPOS, runs for 10 shops, and uses a camera over a green pad so staff can bill like a shopkeeper looking at the counter. Please use the stack below.

Target: Nu 500 per shop per month (YetiPOS lists Nu 999–1,499). Hardware for the camera is once (~Nu 7,000–11,000 per pad). After that, looking at the pad stays inside that Nu 500 — so the camera work runs on the till PC.

Use this for the till (beat Yeti on the floor)

Counter

Barcode focus, qty, Pcs/Pack/Case, GST tax invoice, cash and Bhutan QR on one bill. Keyboard-friendly. Big total. Cashier finishes a sale without a phone call.

Stock and units

Case takes carton pieces. Sale blocked when qty × unit is more than on-hand. Rates from product master so staff do not need the owner at the counter.

  • One ticket screen: lines, unit, qty, Nu, pay.
  • Messages the cashier can read: unknown barcode, need stock, camera unsure.
  • Works on a cheap PC; owner can check on a phone.
  • Camera proposal visible before it becomes a line; cashier can accept or tap another SKU.
